Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines information retrieval with
large language model generation.

A RAG system typically retrieves relevant information from an external
knowledge base before generating an answer.

The main benefits of RAG include access to external knowledge,
better grounding, easier knowledge updates, and the ability to work
with domain-specific information.

RAG also introduces challenges. Retrieval quality strongly affects
answer quality. Systems require document processing, chunking,
embedding generation, indexing, retrieval, and monitoring.

RAG can be an alternative to fine-tuning when the primary requirement
is to provide an LLM with changing or domain-specific information.

Important RAG components include document loaders, text splitters,
embedding models, vector databases, retrievers, and language models.
